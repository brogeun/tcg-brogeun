# Portrait sources

The eight SVGs use Pokémon Dream World vector artwork distributed by https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites under `sprites/pokemon/other/dream-world/{id}.svg`.
IDs: Meowth 52, Eevee 133, Squirtle 7, Pikachu 25, Psyduck 54, Gengar 94, Snorlax 143, Jigglypuff 39.

The original vector path data is retained inside a head crop and circular clipping mask. These are different original illustrations from the earlier 128-pixel Shuffle icons; their expression and pose therefore differ. They are not AI-generated or bitmap upscales. SVG root dimensions are 512×512 and stay scalable; canvas backing resolution follows device pixel ratio.

Pokémon character rights remain with their respective owners. Source availability does not transfer those rights.
